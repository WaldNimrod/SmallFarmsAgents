## Updated interpretation

**Cherry tomato**
I will treat cherry tomato as the same species as tomato, but as a **separate SFA crop entry** whose agronomic values must come from sources that are clearly about **small-fruited indeterminate cherry or cocktail types**. That means generic tomato sources are only acceptable when the field is truly species-level and not meaningfully changed by fruit type.

**Edamame**
I will treat edamame as **fresh-harvest soybean** and will only accept sources that are clearly about **green-pod / fresh soybean harvest** for edamame maturity and harvest fields. Dry-soybean sources remain valid only for the dry soybean entry.

## What this changes in source acceptance

For **cherry tomato**, I will now use this filter:

**Accept**
- Sources explicitly saying **cherry tomato**, **cocktail tomato**, **small-fruited tomato**, or named cherry cultivars such as **Sungold**, **Sweet 100**, or **Black Cherry**
- Tomato sources that are clearly describing **indeterminate fresh-market small-fruited plants**

**Reject**
- Beefsteak, slicer, Roma, plum, paste, processing, or determinate field-tomato guidance when used for cherry-tomato-specific fields

For **edamame**, I will now use this filter:

**Accept**
- Sources explicitly saying **edamame**, **vegetable soybean**, **fresh soybean**, or describing harvest at the **green-pod / R6** stage

**Reject**
- Dry soybean guidance when used for edamame **DTM**, **harvest window**, or other harvest-stage-sensitive fields

## Field-by-field rule I will apply

### Fields that may still use broader species-level sources

These fields are often biologically shared across the species and may still be usable from broader tomato or soybean sources, provided the source is otherwise credible:

- **Germination temperature**
- **Soil pH target**
- **Frost tolerance**
- **Pest-host taxonomy**

### Fields that must now be type- or harvest-stage-specific

These fields are too sensitive to crop form or harvest stage to accept generic tomato or dry-soybean sources:

For **cherry tomato**
- **DTM**
- **Spacing**
- **Yield**
- **Harvest window**
- **Succession interval**
- Any market/handling values that are clearly fruit-type-specific

For **edamame**
- **DTM**
- **Harvest window**
- **Yield**
- **Postharvest handling**
- Any source describing maturity at **dry seed** stage rather than **green pod** stage

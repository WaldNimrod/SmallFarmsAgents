# C2 — Visual DNA (the watercolor line to match)

Source: the Devora masters (`source_masters/`) + the locked
`01-PROMPT-watercolor-backgrounds.md`. This is the binding style.

## Medium (real watercolor)
Washed pigment stains, undefined/feathered edges, visible transparent layers,
**granulation** (pigment pooling darker at edges). Reads as paint **on paper** —
old-paper texture, uncontrolled water blooms. A light pencil under-drawing may show.

## Character
Quiet, gentle, semi-abstract. **Atmosphere > literal accuracy** — the viewer feels
the world (garden, soil, water) without a caption. Warm, humble, handcrafted.

## Reference
The brand's own line (Devora): radishes/lettuce/dill/parsley/bunch + the woven
basket. If the engine needs an external anchor: *"watercolor illustrations in the
style of Lisa Congdon's garden series — gentle, washed, visible paper texture and
pigment pooling."*

## Palette (illustrations)
Cream paper `#f5f3ec` / soil-wash `#eef0e0`; all colors < ~0.5 saturation.
olive `#6a8a3a` · earth `#5b483a` (contours) · tan-orange `#c46a3e` · dusty teal
`#2d8a8c` (sparingly). **No bright red in art.**

## Composition
Single subject or small loose cluster, often partial/cropped (one or two leaves,
not a whole plant). Off-center; one **quiet zone** (~30%) of near-empty paper for
text overlay. Asymmetric, airy.

## Forbidden (anti-patterns)
Vector-flat · digital gradients · clean preserved pencil outline · commercial
"watercolor-brush" illustration · glossy/over-saturated AI watercolor · whole
plants seen from above · drawn people/hands (in backgrounds) · graphically
stylized seeds · text/numbers/logos · 3D · drop shadows · repeated
tomato+carrot+lettuce centered lineups.

# Style DNA — visual analysis of the nimrod.bio illustration line

Derived from direct inspection of the four anchor images in `../images/`.

## Paper tone
Warm cream / off-white with a faint natural-paper grain. Never pure white,
never bright. Matches the live SFA `--gj-paper #f6f1e3`.

## Line quality
Hand-drawn, confident but relaxed. Warm **brown ink/pencil** outline (not black),
slightly broken/sketchy in places (see basket line-art + the radish contours).
Lines describe form loosely; they don't fully enclose every shape.

## Watercolor softness
Loose pigment washes with visible bleed and uneven saturation. Highlights are the
paper showing through (no opaque white). Edges feather; pigment pools at the
bottom of forms (see radishes). Gentle, not crisp.

## Color restraint
Muted, earthy, low-saturation: beet/tomato reds, olive/leaf greens, soft gold,
soil browns. A handful of hues per piece. No neon, no candy colors, no high
contrast.

## Composition habits
Single subject or a small loose cluster, often drifting toward one side with
generous empty paper around it (radishes sit in a corner; basket is centered but
airy). Asymmetric, breathing room, casual placement — like a journal sketch.

## Subject matter
Vegetables and produce (radishes, leafy greens, carrot tops), the woven basket,
garden produce. Domestic, edible, seasonal.

## What to AVOID (anti-patterns)
- Generic "AI watercolor": over-saturated, glossy, symmetrical, every edge crisp.
- Centered icon-like lineups of one-tomato-one-carrot-one-lettuce (the current
  draft prompts lean this way — demote it).
- Opaque white highlights, hard drop shadows, 3D, vector flatness.
- Text, numbers, logos baked into the art.
- Busy full-bleed compositions with no breathing room.

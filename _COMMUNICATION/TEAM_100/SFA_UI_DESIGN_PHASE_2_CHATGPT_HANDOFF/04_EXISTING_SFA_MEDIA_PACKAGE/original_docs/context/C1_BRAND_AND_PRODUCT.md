# C1 — Brand & Product Context

**Product:** SFA — "חקלאות קטנה" (Small Farming). A community AI platform for
**Israel's small-scale organic farming market**. Live at https://sfa.nimrod.bio.

**Audience:** small organic growers, market gardeners, and farm-to-table sellers
in Israel. Practical, hands-in-the-soil people. Hebrew-first, right-to-left.

**Core modules (what the site offers):**
- **ספר גידולים (Crop Book)** — open index of crops, varieties, growing cycles.
- **מחירון (Market Index)** — daily normalized produce price index.
- **מחשבון לחקלאי (Calculator)** — profitability / area / yield planning (beta).
- **תכנון עונה (Season Planner)** — beds, rotation, planting calendar (coming).
- **ניהול לקוחות (Clients)** — produce-box subscriptions, billing (paid).
- **מעקב יבול ומלאי (Inventory)** — harvest, losses, cold storage (paid).
- **חיבור Tend (Tend Bridge)** — invoice/income sync (custom).
- **יומן שדה (Field Log)** — weather, disease, treatment journaling (custom).

**Brand personality:** calm, earnest, crafted, trustworthy. Think a beautifully
kept **garden journal** / "home research workshop" — hand-painted bed maps,
pencil notes, a sprig of herbs on the desk. NOT corporate, NOT techy-neon, NOT
stocky.

**Brand mark:** a simple two-leaf **seedling/sprout** in leaf-green. (Used for
the logo and the favicon.)

**Why illustrations (not photos):** the whole UI is a warm cream "paper" canvas
with watercolor washes and hand-drawn icons. Photos would break the calm,
crafted feel. Every generated asset must sit inside this paper world.

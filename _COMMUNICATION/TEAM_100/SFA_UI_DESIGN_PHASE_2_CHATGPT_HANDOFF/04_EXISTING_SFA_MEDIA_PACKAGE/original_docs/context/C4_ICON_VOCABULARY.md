# C4 — Subject & Icon Vocabulary

To keep the set coherent, draw subjects from the same vegetable/farm vocabulary
the site already uses in its hand-drawn icon set. This keeps the heroes visually
related to the line-icons that sit on each card.

## Established vegetable vocabulary (from the site's icon set)
leaf · seedling/sprout · tomato · lettuce · cucumber · pepper · eggplant ·
carrot · onion · zucchini · basil · leafy greens (chard/kale) · strawberry.

## Per-module subject anchors (what each card's hero should feature)
| Module | Hebrew | Subject anchor | Accent color |
|--------|--------|----------------|--------------|
| crop-book | ספר גידולים | rows of leafy greens / a tidy garden bed (top-down) | leaf-green |
| market | מחירון | open-air market stall, mixed veg in baskets | tomato-red |
| calc | מחשבון | a softly-painted grid/ledger motif + a leaf | sun-gold |
| planner | תכנון עונה | a planting-calendar grid with tiny veg sketches | leaf-green |
| clients | ניהול לקוחות | a farm ledger/notebook beside a produce basket | soil-brown |
| inventory | מעקב יבול ומלאי | a wooden harvest crate of mixed produce | tomato-red |
| tend-bridge | חיבור Tend | abstract connection/bridge motif over a farm field | soil-brown |
| field-log | יומן שדה | an open notebook with crop sketches + weather marks | leaf-green |

## Recurring props for the wider scenes (hub / contact)
hand-painted bed map, wooden ruler, pencil, orange slices, newspaper clipping,
sprig of sage/basil, a small greenhouse, a simple wooden bench.

## The brand mark (for favicon)
A simple **two-leaf seedling/sprout** rising from a short stem, leaf-green
`#6f8a45`, centered on cream paper, generous padding. Clean and iconic at tiny
sizes.

## Consistency rule
Vegetables are stylized watercolor illustrations — not botanical-accurate, not
cartoonish-cute. Same level of detail and the same muted palette across all
assets.

# C1 — Product Context (what SFA is, and where art goes)

**SFA — "חקלאות קטנה"** — a community platform for Israel's small-scale organic
farming market (https://sfa.nimrod.bio). Hebrew-first, RTL. Audience: small
organic growers and market gardeners. Brand: "מהגינה של נימרוד" (From Nimrod's
Garden) — calm, crafted, trustworthy; a garden journal.

## Where images appear (slots)
- **OG share** 1200×630 · **8 module heroes** 800×800 (1:1, full-bleed) ·
  **hub hero** 1600×900 · **contact** 1600×900 · **favicon** · **icons** (line) ·
  plus future: **textures/backgrounds**, **empty states**, **community art**.
- Titles are rendered by HTML (Frank Ruhl Libre, RTL) OVER the art → **no text in images**.

# C2 — Visual DNA (the style to match)

Derived from the nimrod.bio illustration line (see C4 + the attached reference
images). This is the binding style.

## The feeling
Delicate · pleasant · hinted/suggested · calm · quiet · handmade. A garden
journal page, not a brochure.

## Paper & ground
Warm cream `#f6f1e3` with faint natural paper grain. Never pure white, never bright.

## Line
Hand-drawn, relaxed, confident. **Warm brown ink/pencil** (not black). Slightly
broken/sketchy; lines describe form loosely and don't fully enclose every shape.

## Watercolor
Loose washes with visible bleed and uneven saturation. Highlights = paper showing
through (no opaque white). Edges feather; pigment pools at the base of forms.

## Color
Muted, earthy, low-saturation: beet/tomato reds, olive/leaf greens, sun-gold,
soil browns. A few hues per piece. No neon, no candy, no high contrast.

## Composition
Single subject or a small loose cluster, often off-center, with generous empty
paper around it. Asymmetric, airy, casual — a sketch, not a poster.

## Avoid (anti-patterns)
Generic AI watercolor (over-saturated/glossy/symmetrical/crisp) · centered
icon-like one-tomato-one-carrot-one-lettuce lineups · opaque white highlights ·
hard shadows · 3D · vector flatness · text/numbers/logos · busy full-bleed scenes.
